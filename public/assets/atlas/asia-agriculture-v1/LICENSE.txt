Asia rice distribution derivative assets

Source: International Food Policy Research Institute (IFPRI), 2026,
Global Spatially-Disaggregated Crop Production Statistics Data for 2020
Version 2.0 Release 2 (MapSPAM 2020 v2r2).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/SWPENT
Accessed through the CGIAR Climate Action Data Hub:
https://cgiar-climate-data-hub.github.io/catalog/spam2020/
Dataset-specific license declared by that distribution: CC-BY-SA-4.0.

Adaptations by Insight Journal: selected rice, all production systems,
harvested area; subset to three Asia regions; masked to the existing
Natural Earth country outlines; rounded query values to 0.01 ha;
classified and reprojected the display with nearest-cell resampling.
