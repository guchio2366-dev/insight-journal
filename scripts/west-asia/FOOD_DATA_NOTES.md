# West Asia food data check (2026-10-09)

- The retained West Asia FAOSTAT QCL archive is `https://bulks-faostat.fao.org/production/Production_Crops_Livestock_E_All_Data_(Normalized).zip`, SHA-256 `c5835418c18f9322e7decbd6800f93a216eaae3cdfa31acb08f0518c0c6d6853`. The local West Asia `statistics.json` and Europe's retained World rows record the same archive hash. `prepare-world-shares.mjs` matches item code, production element `5510`, unit `t`, and year before dividing the sum of reported values for the 20 editorial targets by the FAOSTAT World row. It produces 2015–2024 shares for wheat, barley, rice, cattle milk, chicken meat, hen eggs, and cattle meat. The World rows retained in the existing Europe extract omit dates, olives, and buffalo milk; their shares remain uncalculated.
- Read-only checks of `https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/TCL` and `https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/QCL` through the web reader on 2026-10-09 returned a JavaScript site shell with **0 readable lines**, not downloadable partner-country or food balance rows. A direct read-only HEAD request for the exact QCL archive above received HTTP 403 from this workspace's CONNECT tunnel. The denied route was not retried. This is a retrieval limitation, not evidence that FAOSTAT lacks the three missing World rows or bilateral trade data.
- FAO's [Detailed trade matrix catalog](https://data.fao.org/catalog/iso/955a7b61-ce56-4a04-adf5-61a3012eec84) identifies partner-country export quantities in tonnes; FAO's [December 2025 release note](https://www.fao.org/statistics/events/events-detail/trade-of-agrifood-products---2000-2024.-december-2025-update/en) says the trade domain covers 2024. Those are promising sources, but they do not supply the actual 20-country, ten-item, 2024 rows in this workspace. Before an export-destination pie chart, obtain the matrix extract, match reporter/partner/item/flow/year/unit, resolve missing reporters and re-exports, and reconcile the partner sum with the corresponding reported export total. A production-weighted destination chart would be a different, invalid measure.
- FAO's [food balance concepts](https://www.fao.org/4/x9892e/x9892e02.htm) and [New Food Balances methodology](https://files-faostat.fao.org/production/FBS/New%20FBS%20methodology.pdf) distinguish production, gross imports, gross exports, stock changes, and domestic utilization. The requested supply and disposition band needs these quantities for one country, product-equivalent definition and year. The saved QCL production series alone cannot fill the band. A later data pass should obtain 2024 national balance rows where available, check item conversions and the 20-country coverage, then label any chart by its exact balance definition; do not mix a 2015–2024 QCL production numerator with a differently classified balance item.
- The three missing world denominators (dates item 577, olives 260, raw buffalo milk 951; production element 5510, tonnes) need to be re-extracted from the **same checksum-identified QCL archive** for each year 2015–2024. Cross-source global approximations or another release would not be comparable with the saved numerator. The source archive is not present locally, and the retained Europe World subset excludes these items. Their shares are pending retrieval, not asserted unavailable in FAOSTAT.
- An earlier trade ZIP attempt was reported as HTTP 403 and an UN Comtrade route was denied, but its exact request URL and response record are not retained in this branch. We do not attribute that status to a guessed URL or retry a denied path. No partner-country import/export, domestic disposition, or food import dependence numbers have been calculated from those attempts.
- QCL national production (`t`) cannot stand in for imports, exports, food available for domestic use, or the country where a food item was obtained. The map's corresponding cards say which quantities are absent from retained data. A trade destination pie chart requires partner quantities with a verified total, while a supply-flow bar also needs an independently defined domestic disposition.
