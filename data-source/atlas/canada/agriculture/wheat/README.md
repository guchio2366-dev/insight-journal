# Canadian all-wheat initial learning package

Scope: 2021 all-wheat distribution; Canada and ten provinces, four annual reference years and three metrics; beginner reading from crop conditions to management, uses, transport, Japan demand and quality institutions. No livestock, forestry, crop-class quantity or export statistics implementation is included. These remain separate unfinished packages.

## Source and reuse ledger

- Statistics Canada, *Total wheat area by census division (CD), 2021*: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/95-634-x/2021001/article/00001/catm-ctra-025-eng.htm . The official article identifies each random dot as 15,000 acres / approximately 6,070 hectares, inside a census division's agricultural area. Dots are area quantities, not farm locations or farm counts. Canada's census total is 9,413,876 ha. Preserve suppression, agricultural ecumene/null and outside-ecumene categories. Do not reverse-engineer exact CD areas from rounded dots.
- Original image: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/95-634-x/2021001/article/00001/m-c/m-c-025-eng.jpg . Crop 1133×875 to `[0,0,1133,814]`, removing official footer symbols. Preserve full map, both Ontario/Quebec insets, legend and source line. JPEG re-encode quality 95; no reprojecting, moving points or changing classes. Original checksum and processing are in `provenance.json`; original is linked, not redistributed. Original and cropped images were visually inspected.
- Statistics Canada table 32-10-0359-01: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3210035901 . Full official CSV https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/tbl/csv/32100359-eng.zip was already acquired for the canola pilot.
