# South Asia: subnational ethnicity and religion source audit (2026-10-13)

## Stored inputs and decision rule

`religion-source-review.md` records six verified national religion profiles and selected province/district examples for other countries. `src/data/atlas/asia-south-central-religion.ts` transcribes those examples. `public/assets/atlas/asia-settlements-v1/manifest.json` identifies the settlement polygons as GeoEPR 2021 and EPR-ED 2021, which describe selected politically relevant groups, not a census of all residents. The repository contains no official ethnicity/religion-by-district source table or licensed matching district boundary for Afghanistan, Bhutan or Maldives. The Natural Earth national outlines used for the basemap do not supply these district geometries.

For a new composition map, require an official table with the exact question/category, reference year, resident universe, geography names/codes, each group count and the total/unknown count for the same geography. Then require boundary geometry for those same units and a redistribution permission. A national estimate, mother tongue, citizenship, nationality or a GeoEPR footprint cannot fill any missing cell. A publication being inaccessible is **not** evidence that its data do not exist.
