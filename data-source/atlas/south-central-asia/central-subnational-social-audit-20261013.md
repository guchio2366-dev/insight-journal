# Central Asia: subnational ethnicity and religion source audit (2026-10-13)

## Stored inputs and interpretation

`religion-source-review.md` and `src/data/atlas/asia-south-central-religion.ts` hold a verified Kazakhstan 2021 national religion profile and non-census descriptions for the other four countries. `public/assets/atlas/asia-settlements-v1/manifest.json` records GeoEPR 2021 / EPR-ED 2021 polygons for selected politically relevant groups. Their boundaries and group-level religious attributes do not represent all residents or the predominant faith in every locality. No official ethnicity/religion-by-locality table and matching licensed small-area boundary for the four countries below is checked into this repository.

An administrative composition map needs counts for every published category, an explicit total/unknown denominator and geography code for the **same reference year and population universe**, plus the corresponding boundary and reuse terms. Mother tongue, citizenship and sampled group membership are different variables. Do not infer a share or fill a missing district from them.
