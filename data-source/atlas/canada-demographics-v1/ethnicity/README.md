# Canada population groups, 2021

This is a bounded selection from Statistics Canada table **98-10-0324-01**, released October 26, 2022 and accessed October 2, 2026. It contains Canada and the 41 complete census metropolitan areas in the atlas. Geographies are matched by official DGUID, with CMA geography level 503 and the original three-digit classification code. Ottawa–Gatineau uses the complete CMA, `2021S0503505`, rather than either provincial part.

The universe is **persons in private households in occupied private dwellings, 2021 Census — 25% Sample data**. These are weighted long-form estimates. The denominator is the table's `Total - Population group`, at total generation status, total age, Count, and total visible minority. The table has no gender dimension and covers all persons. Its denominator differs from the atlas's total population-and-dwelling count. Do not use that other denominator to calculate these percentages.

The **14 display groups are this site's selection of official members**, not an official 14-category classification. The original population-group dimension contains 87 members, including totals, subtotals and detailed categories. The site selects member IDs `3` through `15`, and `87`: the 12 single-response groups (`3`–`14`), the published multiple-response aggregate (`15`), and the Question 24 Indigenous peoples category (`87`). Each displayed count comes directly from its selected official member. In particular, the multiple-response value is the published member `15` subtotal, not a sum synthesized from detailed leaves. The site only collapses the presentation of the original hierarchy; it does not create or rename population groups or adjust their counts.

In the original hierarchy, member `1` (Total - Population group) has immediate children `2` (Single population groups), `15` (Multiple population groups), and `87` (Indigenous peoples). Member `2` has children `3`–`14`. Member `15` has children `16` (Two population groups) and `84` (Three or more population groups); `16` has children `17`–`83`, and `84` has children `85` and `86`. The site's 14 selected members therefore span original hierarchy levels. The manifest preserves their original IDs and the complete original hierarchy.

These selected groups form a partition without counting multiple respondents again in each single group. ID `87` reflects Yes to Question 24, not the separately defined, broader Indigenous identity variable. White is the actual single-response White category; it was not inferred from or renamed from Not a visible minority. **Population group differs from ethnic or cultural origin** and from the derived visible-minority variable.

`selected-original.csv` is the unaltered 124,940-byte official database-loading export, containing 629 cells. StatCan's WDS API and this CSV omit the remaining Drummondville/Japanese cell. `drummondville-display-original.html` records the official table display explicitly returning `0.0`, formatted as `0`, for the Japanese population-group row and total-visible-minority column. The preparation script restores this one official displayed zero in the adopted 630-cell CSV; the manifest records its source and provenance. No missing value is converted to zero without this evidence. Original VECTOR fields are blank and remain blank; WDS reports vectorId 0 for these census coordinates, not a usable time-series vector.

`cube-metadata-original.json` preserves the complete official category hierarchy, geography DGUIDs, data quality flags, total non-response rates and footnotes. The source CSV retains status, symbol, decimals and coordinates. All adopted status and symbol fields are blank, all decimal fields are 0, and one count is 0. Census counts and totals are rounded independently; the preparation audit records each geography's category-sum difference rather than adjusting any original count. The maximum absolute observed difference is 20 persons.

Canada, Montréal, Vancouver, Calgary and Brantford have data quality flag `20000`, indicating that data for one or more incompletely enumerated reserves or settlements are excluded. Other adopted CMAs have flag `00000`. Long-form total non-response rates are recorded per geography. Sampling and non-response affect these estimates; see the official reference guide preserved here.

Run `node scripts/prepare-canada-ethnicity.mjs` from the repository to reproduce the JSON, selected CSV, manifest and audit. It performs no network requests. The original response/request files document source URLs, request dimensions, access times and byte counts. The manifest hashes every adopted original.

The additional official article, *The Canadian census: A rich portrait of the country's religious and ethnocultural diversity*, describes regional diversity and immigration. Its derived racialized/visible-minority counts can include multiple responses differently from the single-response categories here. They must not be substituted for this table's adopted counts.
