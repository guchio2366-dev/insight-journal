# Official census culture cases

This package contains **two selected cases**: England/Wales with 331 LAD2021 areas, and Croatia with one explicitly published national total. It does not cover the whole United Kingdom or Europe. These grains differ and must not be presented as a uniform regional comparison. No electoral data is included.

The selected original [Nomis Census 2021 bulk](https://www.nomisweb.co.uk/sources/census_2021_bulk) CSVs are TS021 (ethnic group) and TS030 (religion), using their lower-tier local authority and original country tables. Their retained metadata identifies version 1, issued 29 November 2022, census reference date 21 March 2021. These extracts must not be described as the latest ONS version. Counts are persons, including all usual residents; each topic's own published total is the denominator.

All original category columns are retained. TS021 has 19 detailed responses and five overlapping aggregates. Add only the 19 detailed responses, never the aggregates and their children together. TS030 preserves No religion separately from Not answered. Ethnicity is self-identification and religion is affiliation, not a measure of ancestry, citizenship, language, practice or belief. Targeted record swapping and cell key perturbation can make topic totals or category sums differ slightly. The package records these differences and does not adjust counts or rescale shares to sum to 100%.

Boundaries are the official [ONS Local Authority Districts (December 2021) UK BUC](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=7b13dd6f2dae42f498ae6f28d42f6703), selected for England/Wales by LAD21CD. BUC is ONS's 500 m ultra-generalised coastline-clipped product. All 331 source CSV codes join exactly to all 331 official feature codes. The official service transforms EPSG:27700 to EPSG:4326. The small selected boundary extract is retained here; public geometry preserves its coordinates and changes only the properties. The package adds no inferred polygons or extra cartographic simplification.

Statistics and boundaries use [OGL v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), with the [ONS geographical licence and attribution requirements](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/geography/licences). Public consumers must show: “Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0” and “Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026”. Source selection, property changes and original hierarchy preservation are declared adaptations; no source endorsement is implied.

`manifest.json` records original download URLs, UTC retrieval times, SHA-256 hashes, exact selected extracts and generated asset hashes. Large original ZIPs remain in a private cache outside this repository. Rebuild offline with `node scripts/europe/prepare-population-cases.mjs`. To repeat extraction from the originally verified ZIPs, add `--cache <private-source-cache>`; the script checks their recorded hashes before extraction. Preparation does not fetch the network, and does not run during the site build.

Croatia uses the **explicit Republic of Croatia national row 9** in the original [official Census 2021 workbook](https://podaci.dzs.hr/media/td3jvrbu/popis_2021-stanovnistvo_po_gradovima_opcinama.xlsx), sheet 1 (ethnicity) and sheet 2 (religion). Both published all-usual-resident denominators are 3,871,833, reference date 31 August 2021. The small retained JSON extract preserves the original bilingual headings, national count cells, published rounded percentages and method notes. The workbook's HTTP Last-Modified is 13 June 2025; this is a file update date, not the census year. It uses the [Croatian Open Licence](https://dzs.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/Dokumenti/Open%20Licence.pdf?vel=244016), as stated by the [Croatian Bureau of Statistics open-data policy](https://dzs.gov.hr/print.aspx?id=1823&url=print). Attribute the Croatian Bureau of Statistics, link the licence and source, retain the update date and identify extraction as an adaptation.

Croatian ethnic response categories are kept separately from the England/Wales hierarchy, including regional/religious affiliation, unclassified, not declared and unknown. Its 12 religion responses retain Catholics, Orthodox, Protestants and Other Christians as separately self-declared, plus distinct agnostics/sceptics, not religious/atheists, not declared and unknown. The original Other Christians footnote remains in the extract and topic notes; religious-community membership must not be used to reclassify their stated religious responses.

The Croatian source attribution includes the licence's fallback notice verbatim: “Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Data Licence”. The same attribution also identifies the adaptations: explicit national rows and original count columns selected; Japanese explanatory headings added; existing Natural Earth country outline selected for display only with unchanged coordinates. Licence and source URLs remain attached to the case and its topics for the public reader.

Croatia's unchanged existing Natural Earth HRV outline is **display-only**, public domain, and not labelled as a 2021 census county boundary. No county distribution is shown or inferred, and counts are not obtained by adding county rows. The optional official 2021 county-boundary case remains blocked: GISCO's download-provisions link returned 404 and its current administrative-unit policy restricts commercial use. No GISCO geometry, county centroid, proxy geometry or assumed county licence is used.
